# Code of Conduct

JWU NCSA is a student club. Everyone who takes part in our repos, events and platforms must follow this code
and the Johnson & Wales University student code of conduct.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or skill level.
- Give and take feedback on work, not on people.
- Help newer members learn.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Attacking systems, accounts or people outside an authorized exercise. Security skills learned here are used
  only on systems you own or have written permission to test.
- Sharing CTF flags or solutions during an active competition.

## Enforcement

Report problems to any club officer (listed on the [organization page](https://github.com/JWU-NCSA)). Officers
will review every report and keep the reporter's identity private. Consequences range from a warning to
removal from the organization and its events.
